## Context 

Financial markets present a complex and dynamic environment, offering a rich source of structured data that can be leveraged for a variety of predictive modeling tasks. In this project, we explore a unified framework that applies three distinct modeling paradigms — **time-series forecasting**, **survival analysis**, and **ranking** — to the historical performance of ten globally recognized stocks.

We focus on large-cap, widely traded equities such as **Apple (AAPL)**, **Microsoft (MSFT)**, **Amazon (AMZN)**, **Google (GOOGL)**, **Meta (META)**. These "blue-chip" stocks are known for their strong market presence and relatively high liquidity, making them ideal candidates for multi-faceted financial modeling.

This project integrates three predictive tasks:

1. **Time-Series Forecasting**
    
    We forecast short-term stock returns using historical price patterns, leveraging statistical and machine learning models. This component targets the classic financial modeling challenge of predicting future asset behavior based on past performance.
    
2. **Survival Analysis**
    
    By reframing financial events as survival outcomes, we model the *time until a significant price movement occurs* — such as a 5% drop or gain. This allows us to understand risk and timing, rather than just direction, which is particularly relevant for managing stop-loss strategies or estimating holding periods.
    
3. **Ranking Models**
    
    We construct a relative ranking of stocks at each time step based on predicted returns or risk-adjusted metrics. This supports portfolio allocation and decision-making strategies that prioritize the most promising assets at any given moment.
